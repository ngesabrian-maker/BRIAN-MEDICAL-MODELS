Who Should Use This App?

Brian Meds is an educational medical learning application intended primarily for students and professionals involved in health sciences and medical education.

  • Medical students (MBChB/MBBS)
  • Clinical medicine and clinical officer students
  • Nursing students and nurses
  • Medical laboratory science students and laboratory personnel
  • Pharmacy students and pharmaceutical personnel
  • Dental and community oral health students
  • Physiotherapy and occupational therapy students
  • Biomedical and health science students
  • Medical interns and healthcare trainees
  • Medical educators, tutors, and lecturers
  • Healthcare professionals seeking a structured study reference
  • Researchers and contributors developing medical educational content

The application is designed to support learning, revision, teaching, and independent study. It is not intended to replace formal medical training, clinical supervision, institutional guidelines, or professional clinical judgment.

How to Use the App

  1. Select a system and organ from the navigation panel.
  2. Choose a subject such as Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, or other available disciplines.
  3. Review the structured study notes provided for the selected system, organ, and subject.
  4. Add your own sticker notes where you need additional personal study reminders.
  5. Open the model chooser to view an available 3D model after explicitly selecting it.
  6. Use the application as a study and revision reference alongside your formal coursework and clinical training.

Educational Purpose

Brian Meds is designed as a medical education and study-support platform. Its purpose is to organize medical knowledge into structured systems, organs, subjects, notes, and interactive learning resources.

The application should be used as a supplementary educational resource and not as a substitute for qualified teaching, institutional protocols, textbooks, clinical guidelines, or professional medical advice.
